## Combination testing

`npm test` exhaustively evaluates all **256** combinations of the four four-choice questions. It also checks each answer value individually, confirms that every rule outcome is explained, and verifies that incompatible standing ability excludes exercises that start or end in standing. A walking-aid answer remains eligible when the standing answer is `standing_support`; it is not blocked by unrelated responses.

## Remaining acceptance gaps

- Nine answer values have no mapped exercise: `leg_assistance`, `leg_limited`, `leg_unable`, `standing_assistance`, `standing_unable`, `walking_assistance`, `walking_unable`, `sit_to_stand_assistance`, and `sit_to_stand_unable`.
- The existing mapped exercises are not fully documented: references/source URLs, contraindications, and stroke-phase applicability are missing from the app catalogue. Therefore a rule match is not evidence of clinical safety or efficacy.
- To close these gaps, add evidence-supported protocols with explicit starting positions, assistance/helper instructions, steps, precautions/contraindications, applicability, and credible citations. Do not populate the empty mappings until those records exist.
- Day sections organize matched exercises for display only; they do not prescribe a rehabilitation schedule or frequency.
